## CORRECTED Exam Pattern (from OCR of actual papers)
**STOP TEACHING CALCULUS-HEAVY MATH. The exam is mostly word problems and reasoning.**

### Math Section — Analytical/Reasoning Word Problems (NOT calculus)
- Profit/loss/commission calculations
- Percentage problems (what % of X are Y)
- Average/arithmetic mean problems
- Ratio and proportion
- Set/Venn diagram problems (French/German class overlap)
- Speed/distance/time problems
- LCM/HCF problems
- Remainder problems
- Sales tax calculations
- Money/spending word problems
- Area percentage change (radius increased by X%)
- Prime numbers
- Integer/algebra word problems
- Age problems

### Physics Section — Mostly Conceptual + Basic Calc
- Who discovered what (quantum theory, relativity)
- Type of motion (periodic, circular, linear)
- Unit prefixes (pico, nano)
- Vector operations (parallel/perpendicular)
- Basic free fall time calculation
- Pendulum (period, length, mass doesn't affect period)
- PE = mgh, KE = ½mv² (basic)
- Wave frequency = 1/T
- Barometer, dew point, troposphere temperature
- Ohm's law V=IR
- Coulomb's law
- Resistance calculation (R = ρL/A)
- Standing waves/nodes
- Scalar vs vector
- Half-life problems
- Electromagnetic wave identification
- Self-inductance

### Chemistry Section — Memorization/Factual
- Electron charge, composite particles
- Rutherford model limitations
- Orbital configurations (s,p,d,f, what's possible)
- Industrial processes (Haber-Bosch)
- Plaster of Paris formula
- Aqueous solution nature (acidic/alkaline/neutral for salts)
- pH + pOH = 14
- Strongest acid identification
- Enthalpy (ΔH)
- Food preservation agents
- SATP conditions
- Mole calculations
- Non-conductors
- Glass color with metal oxides

### English Section
- Reading comprehension (long passages)
- Grammar: sentence correction, prepositions
- Synonyms (vocabulary)
- Fill in the blanks (tenses, verb forms)
- Odd one out
